The person behind the work

Curiosity, structure and a sharp eye for detail.

I’m María Moreno Poncela, a designer from Jaén now based in Madrid. My work moves between understanding people, shaping services and giving ideas a clear visual form.

My background in design and advertising helps me see a project from more than one angle: what people need, what the business needs, and how an experience should feel.
